# Project 2: Developing Incident Response Playbooks

## Project Overview
This project focused on developing structured incident response playbooks to guide an organisation's response to common cybersecurity incidents.

Three playbooks were developed, covering phishing, ransomware, and Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attacks.

This was a collaborative project completed as part of the TechCrush Cybersecurity Capstone, Cohort 8.

## Objectives
- Develop practical incident response procedures for common cyber threats.
- Define the activities required during each incident response phase.
- Establish roles and responsibilities during incident handling.
- Develop communication strategies for incident response.
- Identify metrics for evaluating response effectiveness.
- Create a post-incident review template.

## Incident Scenarios Covered

### 1. Phishing Incident Response Playbook
Developed procedures for identifying and responding to phishing incidents.

The playbook covers:
- Incident detection and identification.
- Containment of affected accounts or systems.
- Removal of malicious emails and related threats.
- Recovery of affected systems and accounts.
- Communication and reporting procedures.

### 2. Ransomware Incident Response Playbook
Developed response procedures for ransomware incidents involving potentially compromised or encrypted systems.

The playbook covers:
- Identifying ransomware activity.
- Containing affected systems to limit further spread.
- Eradicating the threat and addressing its cause.
- Recovering affected systems and data.
- Coordinating communication and response responsibilities.

### 3. DDoS Incident Response Playbook
Developed procedures for responding to Distributed Denial-of-Service attacks that affect service availability.

The playbook covers:
- Detecting unusual traffic and service disruption.
- Containing and mitigating the attack.
- Removing or addressing the source of the disruption.
- Restoring affected services.
- Communicating incident status to relevant stakeholders.

## Incident Response Framework
Each playbook follows four major response phases:

1. **Detection:** Identify and assess the incident.
2. **Containment:** Limit the impact and prevent further damage.
3. **Eradication:** Remove the threat and address its cause.
4. **Recovery:** Restore affected systems and services.

The playbooks also define response roles, communication procedures, and metrics.

## Post-Incident Review
A post-incident review template was developed to document:
- What happened.
- The impact of the incident.
- Response actions taken.
- What went well and what could be improved.
- Root cause.
- Recommended follow-up actions.

## Key Learning Outcomes
- Understanding structured incident response processes.
- Developing incident-specific response procedures.
- Understanding containment, eradication, and recovery.
- Documenting roles and communication responsibilities.
- Preparing post-incident reviews and improvement actions.
- Improving cybersecurity documentation and response planning skills.
